# Ankit Mukherjee, MSc
### Computational biology · Human genetic variation · Disease transcriptomics

I work at the intersection of population genomics, pharmacogenomics, and transcriptomics, connecting computational analyses with biological questions and careful interpretation.

My CV records my role as **Project Research Scientist I at CSIR–Institute of Genomics and Integrative Biology**, following work as Project Associate I. My research spans GenomeIndia population variation and pharmacogenomics, Friedreich ataxia blood and cardiomyocyte transcriptomics, and SCA12-associated transcript regulation.

## Technical and interpretive strengths

- **Programming:** Python, R/Bioconductor, Bash, and Perl, supported by [algorithm implementations](https://github.com/AnkitMukherji/Bioinformatics-Algorithms), [sequence programming](https://github.com/AnkitMukherji/Python-for-Genomics), and [structural calculations](https://github.com/AnkitMukherji/Perl-Codes).
- **Genomics:** variant processing, annotation, population comparisons, pharmacogenomic evidence integration, and haplotype analysis.
- **Transcriptomics:** bulk and single-cell analysis, differential expression, pathway enrichment, WGCNA, and clinical associations.
- **Scientific reasoning:** inspect confounding and model assumptions, distinguish exploratory associations from mechanisms, and connect numerical outputs to biological context.
- **Research practice:** HPC/SLURM, Git, environment management, scientific visualization, and mentorship, as documented in the CV.
